IMPORTANT CAVEAT — R² INTERPRETATION:
----------------------------------------
The test R²=0.7071 here reflects a RANDOM 80/20 split.
This splits within storms, so training and test observations from
the same storm can appear in both sets. Consecutive track points
for a storm are highly autocorrelated — this inflates R² artificially.
03_advanced_eda.py uses GroupKFold (by storm_id) which prevents this
and gives a more honest out-of-storm generalization estimate.
SHAP importance rankings here reflect which features the model learned
to exploit, but do not confirm causal relationships.
For production use, average SHAP values across multiple random seeds
to assess stability of importance rankings.
